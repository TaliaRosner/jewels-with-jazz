My Bespoke Process

STEP 1

Let's bring your vision to life.

I start by getting to know you and your ideas. Whether you have a clear vision or just inspiration, I'll guide you through the process and help you create a piece that's uniquely yours. The more details you share, the better I can bring your dream jewelry to life. Once we're aligned on a design, I'll provide you with a quote.

STEP 2

We design your piece together.

After a deposit is placed, I begin designing your piece with care and intention. This is a collaborative process—I'll share sketches, computer renders and mock-ups so we can refine every detail together. Your input shapes the design, and I won't move forward until you're completely in love with it.

STEP 3

Your jewelry is brought to life.

Once the design is finalized, it is sent to my team of expert bench jewelers and setters who handcraft and assemble your piece with precision and care. Each piece is carefully inspected to meet my standards before it is beautifully packaged and delivered straight to your door.
